AI-Assisted Location Creation (Optional)

AI is not required.

You can always create and manage PEV charging locations manually — entering all details yourself. The app works fully without AI features.

When enabled, AI makes the process quicker by automatically generating location data from your photos:

  • Charger type identification — detects the charger make and model
  • Socket/plug detection — identifies connector types from photos
  • Operating hours — reads hours from signage in your photos
  • Location details — extracts access info, fees, and other details

You always review and refine the AI-generated information before submitting. Nothing is saved without your confirmation.

This requires a free OpenRouter.ai account. You can set this up later from Settings at any time.

Comparison: With vs Without AI

How location creation differs:

With AI enabled:

  1. Take a photo of the charger
  2. AI analyses and pre-fills all fields
  3. Review & refine the generated data
  4. Confirm and save

Without AI (manual):

  1. Take a photo of the charger (optional)
  2. Manually enter all location details
  3. Select socket types, access, fees, etc.
  4. Confirm and save

Both workflows produce the same result — AI just speeds up the data entry.
