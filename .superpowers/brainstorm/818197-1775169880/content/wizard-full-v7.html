Welcome to PEVPlugShare

PEVPlugShare helps you find and share charging locations for Personal Electric Vehicles (PEVs) — also known as Personal Mobility Devices (PMDs) — such as e-scooters, e-bikes, electric skateboards, and other light electric vehicles.

The app uses OsmAnd to store and sync your charging locations, and OpenRouter for optional AI-powered features.

This wizard will guide you through 9 steps to get everything set up:

  1. Install OsmAnd (map & navigation app)
  2. Create an OsmAnd Cloud account & log in
  3. Create a favourites folder for your charging locations
  4. Sync your folder to OsmAnd Cloud
  5. Connect your OsmAnd account to this app
  6. Verify your folder is available
  7. Set up AI features & choose a model (optional)
  8. Review & finish

Already have OsmAnd set up? Skip straight to the login screen.

Step 3: Log into OsmAnd

Step 3 of 9 — OsmAnd Login

Step 3 of 9 — Log into OsmAnd

Log into OsmAnd

You need an OsmAnd Cloud account so your PEV charging locations can sync between OsmAnd and PEVPlugShare. If you don't have one yet, you can create a free account inside the OsmAnd app.

In the OsmAnd app:

  1. Open OsmAnd on your device
  2. Tap the menu (☰) button
  3. Tap Settings
  4. Tap OsmAnd Cloud
  5. Sign in with your existing account, or tap Create account to register

Tip: Remember the email and password you use here — you'll need the same credentials in Step 6 to connect PEVPlugShare.

Step 4: Create Favourites Folder

Step 4 of 9 — Create Folder

Step 4 of 9 — Create Favourites Folder

Create a Favourites Folder

PEVPlugShare needs a dedicated favourites folder in OsmAnd to store the PEV charging locations you discover and share. This must be a separate folder from the default "Favourites" — it keeps your charging location data organised and prevents the app from modifying your personal OsmAnd favourites.

In the OsmAnd app:

  1. Tap My Places (★ star icon)
  2. Go to the Favourites tab
  3. Tap the + button to add a new favourite point
  4. In the folder / group field, tap to create a new folder
  5. Give it any name you'll recognise — e.g. "PEV Charging", "My Chargers", "PMD Charging Spots"
  6. Save the temporary favourite point (you can delete it afterward)

Why a separate folder? PEVPlugShare reads and writes to this folder via the OsmAnd Cloud API. Using a dedicated folder ensures your personal OsmAnd favourites are never modified. You can name it whatever you like — you'll select it from a list after connecting your account.

Step 5: Sync to Cloud

Step 5 of 9 — Cloud Sync

Step 5 of 9 — Sync to Cloud

Sync to OsmAnd Cloud

Your new favourites folder currently only exists locally on your device. You need to sync it to OsmAnd Cloud so that PEVPlugShare can access it via the API.

In the OsmAnd app:

  1. Tap the menu (☰) button
  2. Tap Settings
  3. Tap OsmAnd Cloud
  4. Tap Sync now
  5. Wait for the sync to complete — look for the green checkmark

Why sync now? PEVPlugShare connects to OsmAnd Cloud (not the local app) to read your folders. If the folder hasn't been synced, it won't appear when you connect your account.

We connected to your OsmAnd Cloud account successfully, but couldn't find any favourites folders other than the default "Favourites".

This usually means one of two things:

  2. Tap My Places (★ star icon)

Step 8: AI Features & Model Selection (Optional)

Step 8 of 9 — AI Overview

Step 8 of 9 — AI Features (Optional)

AI-Assisted Location Creation (Optional)

AI is not required.

You can always create and manage PEV charging locations manually — entering all details yourself. The app works fully without AI features.

When enabled, AI makes the process quicker by automatically generating location data from your photos:

  • Charger type identification — detects make and model
  • Socket/plug detection — identifies connector types
  • Operating hours — reads hours from signage
  • Location details — extracts access info, fees, etc.

You always review and refine the AI-generated information before submitting. Nothing is saved without your confirmation.

Requires a free OpenRouter.ai account. You can set this up later from Settings at any time.

Step 8 — Model Selection

Step 8 of 9 — AI Features (Optional)

Choose an AI Model

PEVPlugShare uses a vision-capable AI model to analyse your charger photos and generate location details. Different models offer different trade-offs between cost and quality.

Why does the model matter?

The model determines how accurately photos are analysed, how detailed the generated descriptions are, and how well socket types and operating hours are identified. Better models produce more accurate and complete results with fewer corrections needed.
