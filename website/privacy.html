Privacy

Your data stays on your device. PEV PlugShare stores charging locations, photos, and notes in a local database on your Android device. The app works with no network connection and no account.

Optional cloud sync

If you choose to enable cloud sync, authentication uses a standard OAuth 2.0 flow with your chosen provider (for example OpenRouter). The app never sees or stores your provider password; it receives an access token through the browser and stores only what is needed to sync your own locations. You can disconnect at any time from the menu.

What is not collected

  • No advertising identifiers and no ad networks.
  • No third-party analytics or crash trackers.
  • No sale or sharing of location data with data brokers.

Photos

Photos you attach to a location stay in the app's storage and are included in your own backups. EXIF metadata (including coordinates when present) is read to keep photos associated with the right location; it is not transmitted anywhere unless you enable sync and sync that location.

Sharing

When you explicitly share a location (for example via Android's share sheet or by syncing to OsmAnd), that location's details leave the app under the terms of the app or service you share to. Nothing is shared automatically with other PlugShare users.

Source code

PEV PlugShare is open source. You can review exactly what the app does at github.com/githuba42r/PEVPlugShare.
