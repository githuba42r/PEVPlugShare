Privacy Policy for PEV PlugShare

Last Updated: March 8, 2026

Overview

PEV PlugShare ("the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information.

Information Collection and Use

Data Stored Locally

The App stores the following data locally on your device only:

  • Charging Locations: EV charging station locations and details you save or add
  • Photos: Images of charging stations captured or imported within the App
  • App Settings: Your preferences and configuration settings
  • Authentication Tokens: OAuth tokens used to authenticate with the PEV PlugShare service, stored securely on your device

All data is stored exclusively on your device or transmitted only to the PEV PlugShare service you are authenticated with. We do NOT sell or share this data with third parties.

Data We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store on remote servers, or transmit:

  • Personal identification information beyond what is required for authentication
  • Device identifiers
  • Usage analytics to external services
  • Crash reports to third parties
  • Any other personal or sensitive user data to external servers beyond the PEV PlugShare service

Permissions Used

The App requires the following permissions to function:

  • Location (GPS): To display your position on the map and find nearby charging locations
  • Camera: To capture photos of charging stations for upload to the PEV PlugShare service
  • Internet: To sync charging location data with the PEV PlugShare service
  • Network State: To check connectivity before attempting to sync data
  • Notifications: To display sync status and background operation updates
  • Media Location: To read location metadata from photos imported from your device's gallery

Location data is used only to display the map and find nearby stations. It is not transmitted to any external servers.

Third-Party Services

The App uses OpenStreetMap for mapping functionality. When using map features, map tile data is downloaded from OpenStreetMap servers. Please review OpenStreetMap's privacy policy at wiki.osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy.

The App does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services.

Data Security

All user data is stored locally on your device using Android's secure storage mechanisms:

  • Room database for charging location data
  • Android EncryptedSharedPreferences for authentication tokens and sensitive settings
  • Standard Android security practices

Data Retention and Deletion

You can delete your data at any time:

  • Individual charging locations can be deleted from within the App
  • All app data can be deleted by uninstalling the app or clearing app data in Android settings
  • Authentication tokens can be removed by logging out of the App

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

Summary

In simple terms:

  • Your charging location data stays on your phone or is synced only with the PEV PlugShare service you log in to
  • We don't sell your data or send it to advertisers
  • Camera is used only for photos you choose to take of charging stations
  • Location is used only to show you nearby charging stations on the map
  • You can delete all your data anytime
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics
