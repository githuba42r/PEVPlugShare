Every wall socket is a charging station — if you can find it.

PEV PlugShare is an open-source Android app for personal electric vehicles — unicycles, scooters, e-bikes. Record the ordinary GPO outlets that actually charge your ride: where they are, how many sockets, whether they're free, and what to expect when you get there. Works fully offline; syncs with OsmAnd for turn-by-turn navigation.

Get the app See it work

What it does

Share real charging spots

Add, edit, and share ordinary power-point locations that work for PEVs — socket type, count, cost, hours, and notes from the last rider who plugged in.

OsmAnd navigation

One tap hands any location to OsmAnd for turn-by-turn navigation, and syncs your saved spots into OsmAnd's favourites.

Works offline

Everything lives in a local database. No signal, no problem — your locations are on the device wherever you ride.

Photos with context

Photograph the socket, the wall, the landmark you'll look for. EXIF metadata is extracted so photos stay pinned to the right spot, with a full-screen gallery viewer.

Status at a glance

Each card shows what matters before you detour: socket count, public or private, free or paid, 24/7 or business hours.

AI-assisted notes

Optional AI-generated descriptions help fill in details about a location — you stay in control of what gets saved.

Favourites & categories

Heart the spots you rely on, organise by category, and filter the list down to what's useful right now.

Optional cloud sync

Keep your library in sync across devices with standards-based OAuth 2.0 — no account required if you'd rather stay local.

Screens

The list is the home screen: distance, sockets, access, cost, hours — everything you need to pick a spot.

How riders use it

  1. Find. Open the list or map and look for a spot with the sockets your vehicle needs — AU GPO (AS3112) today, more types as the community adds them.
  2. Navigate. Hit the navigation arrow and OsmAnd takes you there, offline maps and all.
  3. Share. Plugged in successfully? Your notes and photos help the next rider. Found a new socket? Add it while the details are fresh.

Open source. Local first. Built for riders.

PEV PlugShare is free and open source. Grab an APK from GitHub, read the code, or open an issue when you find a socket worth sharing.
