AI integration

Let the photos do the paperwork.

Optional, on-device-initiated AI features handle the tedious parts of logging a charging spot — reading socket details straight out of your photos, writing standard descriptions, and turning scribbled notes into structured fields. Powered by OpenRouter with a key from your own account; free models work fine.

What it can do How to turn it on

Photo analysis — sockets, ratings, warnings

Add up to five photos of a spot and let the model read them: socket count and types, where the sockets are located (wall, pillar, splashback…), power rating if it's visible on the plate, and warnings like "customers only", "weathered", or "may be turned off". You can draw focus rectangles on a photo to point the analysis at the outlet plate. Results land in a review dialog — you approve before anything is applied. A brand-new location with photos and an empty description gets analysed automatically.

Description generation — the standard format

Generates the structured description the app and OsmAnd sync expect: a one-or-two sentence opener, then a Details: block covering public access, sockets, hours, cost, extra information, and type. Used when you sync a location to OsmAnd — with an optional "verify description before syncing" checkbox so you can review first. If AI is off or fails, a programmatic template in the same format fills in, so sync never breaks.

Description parser — text into fields

Paste or write an existing description and tap Parse description: opening hours, socket count, capacity, free/paid, public/restricted, and notes are extracted and auto-filled into the form. Perfect for turning a forum post or a note-to-self into proper location data.

Your key, your models

Link an OpenRouter account (or pick up a free tier key) and choose from OpenRouter's full model catalogue — the picker has live search and a free-models-only filter. Free options like Gemini 2.0 Flash, Llama 4 Scout, and Qwen 2.5 VL are plenty for photo reading; paid models (Claude Sonnet 4, GPT-4o, Gemini 2.5 Pro) are there when you want them. The selected model is used for all three features.

Turning it on: AI features are hidden until you configure them. Run the first-time setup wizard (AI step), or open menu → AI Settings: the app opens the OpenRouter authorization page in your browser, you approve, and the app receives an API key via its OAuth callback — nothing to copy or paste. Pick a model, and the AI buttons appear. AI Settings → Disconnect clears the key at any time.

Privacy

AI is entirely optional — the app works fully without it. When you use it, your photos and location descriptions are sent directly from your device to OpenRouter under your own key, for the single request you triggered. No AI provider or analytics SDK is embedded in the app, and nothing is sent in the background. See the privacy policy.
